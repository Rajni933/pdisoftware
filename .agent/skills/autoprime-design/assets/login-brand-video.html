What changed from your reference, and why

Kept

The split, with brand content on the left and the form on the right.
The translucent card, the blue, the rounded corners, the soft depth.
White inputs sitting on the glass, dark committed Sign in button.
Forgot password as a quiet link under the password field.

Changed

Glass no longer sits over the video — contrast would pass on one frame and fail on the next.
Google, GitHub and Facebook buttons removed — this system has no social sign-in.
"Register for free" removed — administrators create accounts, there is no self-signup.
Email field became Employee ID, in mono, matching how staff actually sign in.
Decorative floating blobs dropped — the video is the only movement on the page.

This screen is a documented exception to the design system's no-glass and no-gradient rules, scoped to /signin only and recorded as ADR-009. It carries no operational data and is seen once a shift. It does not travel to any other screen.
