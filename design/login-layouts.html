Locked out

This account is locked
Too many failed sign-in attempts. Try again in 4:58, or ask your branch administrator if you need access sooner.

No retry button while locked. A disabled button people press anyway is worse than no button. The form returns on its own at zero.

Offline

You're offline. Sign in needs a connection — an inspection already in progress keeps working.

This is the only hard network dependency in the whole mobile app. Say so plainly instead of failing on submit.

Session expired during app lock

Your session expired while the app was locked. Sign in to return to inspection PDI-2026-0914.

Biometric unlocks the app; it does not authenticate to the server. When the session is gone, say why and keep the destination.

What makes this login and not a template

Always

One form component, three layout shells — they cannot drift apart later.
Same message and same timing for an unknown ID and a wrong password.
Attempt counter and lockout countdown come from the server, live.
Environment, version and support line under every form.
Real <form>, real autoComplete, Enter submits — password managers must work.

Never

A split-screen hero with a photograph or an illustration.
"Remember me" — device registration already handles persistence.
A self-serve password reset flow. The admin resets passwords.
Naming which field was wrong, or a strength meter on sign-in.
A full-page spinner overlay. The button carries the loading state.
